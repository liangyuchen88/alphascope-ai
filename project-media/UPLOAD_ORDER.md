# Devpost image gallery

Upload the PNGs inside `upload/` in the numbered order below. Use `01-cover.png` as the first image.

All images are 1800 × 1200 (3:2), PNG, and below 5 MB. Ten images total, within the 15-image limit.

Images use actual application screenshots or recorded, source-backed data. They are not AI-generated product mockups. Images 03 and 09 are readable visualizations of recorded replay/quote-audit exports. Portfolio values are hypothetical; forecasts and public quotes are not profit or execution guarantees.

## Upload order and English captions

### 01-cover.png

AlphaScope turns source-backed market research into an interactive journey through real historical storms. Build a portfolio, question the evidence and explore alternative decisions.

### 02-storm-replay.png

Storm Replay lets users navigate a real historical market window with a hypothetical portfolio, delayed execution and trading costs.

### 03-three-routes.png

A recorded replay compares user decisions, the initial allocation held, and a fixed risk rule. Returns, drawdowns and costs are visible; the example does not establish future superiority.

### 04-transmission-map.png

Transmission Map connects sourced events to possible economic mechanisms, counterevidence and observed market windows. Its arrows are unestimated hypotheses.

### 05-risk-radar.png

Risk Radar explains volatility, concentration, drawdown and data-quality flags. Review acknowledgments retain the warning; no automatic orders are placed.

### 06-global-markets.png

Global Markets follows ten representative major-market indices, including the US, UK, Japan, China and Hong Kong. It is a geographic watchlist, not a size ranking.

### 07-strategy-lab.png

Strategy Lab reports cost-aware performance against a matched-period benchmark. This example also makes underperformance visible rather than hiding it.

### 08-forecast-lab.png

Forecast Lab exposes uncertainty and historical errors. Most tested configurations did not beat the no-change benchmark; no profitable trading edge is claimed.

### 09-fx-arbitrage.png

FX & Arbitrage checks both fiat triangles using public bid/ask snapshots, assumed fees/slippage, timing and top-level capacity. Candidates remain execution-unverified.

### 10-politics-and-markets.png

Politics & Markets compares sourced election dates with descriptive asset, sector and VIX observations. Unfinished future windows stay explicitly partial.

## Rebuild

Run `tools/build_project_media.cjs` with an installed Playwright/Chrome environment. `manifest.json` records source files, dimensions, file sizes, captions and SHA-256 hashes. The original screenshots and observations are preserved.
